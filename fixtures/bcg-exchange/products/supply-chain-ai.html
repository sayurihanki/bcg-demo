Supply Chain AI

A modular digital supply-chain offering spanning network design, planning, resilience, control towers, and logistics.

View on BCG.com
Overview

Supply Chain AI combines digital assets, supply-chain expertise, and data science to transform strategy, design, planning, and execution. Specialized modules can be combined around the highest-value constraints and opportunities.

2–4pp

potential EBITDA boost

Capability 01

Network design

Optimize the footprint, flows, inventory allocation, and transportation choices across the network.

Capability 02

End-to-end planning

Connect demand, supply, and financial planning with AI-supported decisions and scenarios.

Capability 03

Digital twin

Model the value chain to test tradeoffs, predict disruption, and improve operational response.

Capability 04

Control tower

Create cross-functional visibility, prioritized alerts, and orchestration across execution teams.
