Smart Banking AI

A unified customer-intelligence platform that equips relationship managers with high-value leads and next-best actions.

View on BCG.com
Overview

Smart Banking AI consolidates customer information into a unified platform and applies AI to help banks serve the right customer with the right action. It connects insight generation directly to relationship-manager workflows.

potential profit boost

Generative AI

Capability 02

Reveal changing customer needs and identify products or services most likely to resonate.

Capability 03

Retention
