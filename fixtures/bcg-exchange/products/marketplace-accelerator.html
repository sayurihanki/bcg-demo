Marketplace Accelerator

AI-powered services that accelerate marketplace launches, drive scale, optimize seller performance, and grow GMV.

View on BCG.com
Overview

Marketplace Accelerator is a series of AI-powered services for launching a new marketplace proposition or expanding an existing one. It combines buyer experience, seller performance, and operations into one value-led transformation path.

AI-powered

launch, scale, and optimization

Capability 01

Marketplace strategy

Define the proposition, operating model, and value pools that make the marketplace worth building.

Capability 02

Buyer experience

Apply AI-driven discovery and personalization to improve relevance, conversion, and loyalty.

Capability 03

Seller acceleration

Improve onboarding, selection quality, pricing, and seller performance with data-led workflows.

Capability 04

Operations cockpit

Connect marketplace health, GMV performance, and execution signals in a single operating view.

Outcomes
  • Faster proposition launch

  • Scalable marketplace operations
