Frontline Ops AI

An AI platform connecting labor, inventory, scheduling, and frontline workflows to improve execution at scale.

View on BCG.com
Overview

Frontline Ops AI connects data, business rules, AI agents, and human decision-makers across labor, inventory, and field workflows. It is designed to improve execution without requiring a full technology replacement.

5%+

labor productivity gains

Capability 01

Labor optimization

Forecast demand, build healthier rosters, and align schedules to business and employee needs.

Capability 02

Inventory flow

Improve availability, surface root causes, and target interventions that protect working capital.

Capability 03

Workforce assistant

Place answers, recommendations, alerts, and task support inside everyday frontline workflows.

Capability 04

Agentic orchestration

Coordinate decisions across systems, rules, AI agents, and accountable human operators.

Outcomes
  • Higher labor productivity
  • Lower employee turnover
  • Fewer schedule edits
  • Better frontline execution
